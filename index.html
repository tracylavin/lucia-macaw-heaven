A little note about gifts

Gifts & Giving

Many of you have been asking about gifts for Lucia! She loves art, learning, and anything to do with macaws—including the idea of helping protect these beautiful birds.

She's especially excited that supporting macaw conservation might mean symbolically adopting a macaw and following its journey. If you'd like to explore that option, here are two wonderful organizations close to her heart:

World Parrot Trust ↗ Macaw Recovery Network ↗

Of course, gifts are never expected. Celebrating together is what matters most!

Both organizations are independent third parties. Any donations or symbolic adoptions are arranged directly through their websites, not through this birthday page. Availability of adoption and follow-up options may vary.
